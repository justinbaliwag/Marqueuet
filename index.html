What is Marqueuet?

Social listening,
without the scrolling.

Marqueuet is a competitive-intelligence workspace for social media. Name two brands or products, and it turns 30 days of public TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook conversation into one side-by-side read—built for desktop deep-dives and mobile checks.

01

Ask in one prompt

Type a matchup like Brand A vs Brand B or benchmark up to three rivals at once. Marqueuet keeps comparisons equal: brand vs brand, product vs product.

02

Read 30 days of public posts

Trailing 30-day public posts across TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, filtered above 5 engagements so you see real signal, not noise.

03

Get a decision-ready view

Share of Voice, sentiment against 24h velocity, community depth, and five purchase drivers. Bookmark it, export a CSV, or print a pitch PDF.
